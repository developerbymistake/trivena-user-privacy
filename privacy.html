Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 August 2026 · Applies to the Trivena app

This Privacy Policy explains how Trivena ("we", "our", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use the Trivena app to browse and book banquet halls, resorts, and travel experiences.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: your name, mobile number, and email (optional) — collected when you sign in via mobile OTP.
  • Location: your approximate or precise location, used to show you nearby venues and districts. You can deny location access and search manually instead.
  • App activity: the venues you shortlist, and a record of when you tap Call or WhatsApp to contact a venue owner.

How We Use Your Information

We use this information to operate the app: showing you relevant venues and connecting you with owners when you choose to make contact. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

Sharing Your Information

When you tap Call or WhatsApp on a venue listing, your contact happens directly between you and the venue owner through your phone's own dialer or WhatsApp app — Trivena does not intercept or store the content of that call or chat. Owners can see that a contact was made (and, if you're logged in, that it came from your account) so they can follow up.

Data Retention

We keep your account information for as long as your account is active. You can delete your account at any time — see Delete Your Account.

Your Rights

You can review and update your profile information from within the app, and request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

Contact Us

Questions about this policy? Email us at support@banquethall.app.

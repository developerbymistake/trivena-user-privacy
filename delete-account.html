Delete Your Account

You can request deletion of your Trivena account and associated data at any time, directly from within the app — no separate form or wait required.

Steps

  1. Open the Trivena app and log in.
  2. Go to Profile.
  3. Tap Delete Account.
  4. Type DELETE to confirm, then tap the confirm button.
What gets deleted: your account is deactivated and you are immediately signed out of all devices. If you'd like your account fully removed rather than deactivated, or have trouble accessing the app, email us at supporttrivena@gmail.com from the email/number associated with your account and we'll take care of it.

See our Privacy Policy for more on how we handle your data.
